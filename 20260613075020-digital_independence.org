#+title: Digital Independence

There are a lot of projects especially by the Free Software Foundation Europe to
gouvern digial self-souvereignability of users, politics and states.
The principles are well-known from topics before the internet and
digitalisation.
There is the goal to prevent monopolies by companies, distribute the power of
the press with diverse public and private options and enable rights like [[id:40e9cc38-ea86-4cf5-aea5-275873c26885][Privacy]]
and free speech to defend the will of the people and freedom in a democratic
system.
Big Tech companies are getting in the way of these goals by Vendor lock-in,
social media powered by algorithms and business models centered around the
collection of user data.
This often involves not only the user, but also linked friends and family
members.

* The network effect and Vendor lock-in
Vendor lock-in is a practice often applied, because the winner will take it all.
This is the network effect of Facebook, Whatsapp, TikTok, X, Office365 and
YouTube.
The effect is simple, because it's very conveniant to stay at your first Mail
provider without changing all the account data for example and on most social
media platforms there is no possibility to export your user data to switch to
another platform.
There are free software alternatives, which try to prevent these mechanisms by
relying on open standards/protocols to enable application diversity like
[[https://jointhefediverse.net/][Fediverse]], [[https://matrix.org/][Matrix]] or [[https://xmpp.org/][XMPP]].
The latter was already there before Whatsapp and the closed version of XMPP by
Whatsapp was made easy to use and conveniant and the network effect plus Vendor
lock-in made it so successfull.
There are a lot of closed implementations of XMPP e.g. Google Talk, Facebook
Messenger and gaming chat platforms.

There is the impression that solutions like [[https://www.libreoffice.org/][Libre Office]] or even [[id:507abdf6-b785-4b29-9271-c365c5fe1a6c][GNU/Linux]]
as a whole are not compatible or not interoperable.
This is most of the time by design and this means that in this case Microsoft
has an interest to bound their users to their applications by using their own
protocols and formats like .docx.
Other companies will rely on the widely used software like Microsoft Teams to be
in contact with other companies, which are using these tools.
Companies are developing applications and drivers for an operating system like
Windows, because this is where the most money for consumer products and desktop
users can be made.
Since this software is often proprietary, it is not always possible to port it
to different operating systems by other parties.

This is also the case for Android and iPhone smartphones.
The app store of iPhones were always a lot more restricted and controlled by
Apple to serve their interest as an important distribution platform for a lot of
companies.
The Android eco-system was more free in terms of the possibilities to install
apps from different sources.
However, the goal of Google is to reduce this user freedom by adding
restrictions for the app developers and app stores in the name of security, but
the true reasons in this regard are often mainly focused on profit maximisation.
This is also the reason to lock down device drivers for Pixel hardware and
delaying information about bugfix patches to make the job of custom Android
operating systems on top of the [[https://source.android.com/][Android Open Source Project]] like
[[https://grapheneos.org/][Graphene OS]] harder.

The usage of Free software, open protocols, free operating systems and cloud
services is often the key for interoperability and the prevention of lock-in and
dependencies on specific service providers, but to switch there is often the
requirement to leave something/somebody behind caused by the network effect and
Vendor lock-in.

** [[https://di.day/en][DiDay]]
There are multiple initiatives, which try to fix these dependencies on Big Tech
companies.
The DiDay project is meant as a starting point to get rid of some services one
at a time, because this can be done quickly without overwhelming users.

Some usefull applications can be found in the [[id:632e3870-2dc8-44bd-b878-979c0072187c][Free Software]] wiki, which do not
all rely on switching to [[id:507abdf6-b785-4b29-9271-c365c5fe1a6c][GNU/Linux]] like tools for office work e.g. [[https://www.firefox.com][Firefox]],
[[https://www.thunderbird.net][Thunderbird]] and [[https://www.collaboraonline.com/][Collabora]].
There are also cloud services like [[https://proton.me/][Proton]], which offer easy, privacy-friendly
alternatives to complete big-tech solutions.
For mail, calendar, address and drive, there is [[https://tuta.com/][Tuta]] and for a focus on mail and
calendars, there are [[https://posteo.de/][Posteo]] and [[https://login.mailbox.org/][Mailbox]].
A completely self-hosted cloud solution can be realised using [[https://nextcloud.com/][Nextcloud]].

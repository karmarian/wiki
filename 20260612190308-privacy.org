#+title: Privacy

There is no freedom without privacy, because privacy is a fundamental human
right to preserve free speech and protection from the gouvernment and big
companies.
As there is no true [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_through_obscurity][Security through obscurity]], there is also no privacy without
[[id:632e3870-2dc8-44bd-b878-979c0072187c][Free Software]].

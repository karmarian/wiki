#+title: Upcycling Android

Billions of smartphones are wasted every year.
Most of them are fully functional.
Manufacturers do not provide long-term software updates.
Mandatory maintenance support improves the situation currently.

On the other side, Google starts to lock-down its app store eco-system like
Apple, because the latter shows that the pressure is not too high to open the
access to other parties.
This results in vendor-lockin and hard dependencies, which breaks [[id:8c32cdfa-a145-4a17-8d71-80e9d480fcdd][Digital
Independence]].
Alternative app stores like [[https://f-droid.org/][F-Droid]] are in danger by this development.
The [[https://auroraoss.com/][Aurora Store]] is an alternative to use the Google PlayStore without a Google
account.

Android is based on the [[https://source.android.com/][Android Open Source Project]] (AOSP), which is using a
fork of the Linux kernel.
In the end, Linux is in a lot of pockets without the knowledge of their owners.
There are multiple operating systems based on the AOSP, which provide more
life-time, freedom, privacy or securtiy.
The Android user interface and services are proprietary products by Google.
Most of the alternative operating systems are deGoogled and do not use the
proprietary apps and services by Google by default.
On some of these operating systems, an alternative implementation of these
services without some of the privacy flaws is shipped as [[https://github.com/microg][MicroG]].
The functionality of specific apps might be rated in the community crowdsourced
compatibility list for deGoogled devices in the [[https://plexus.techlore.tech/][Plexus]] app.

* AOSP based mobile operating systems
** [[https://lineageos.org/][Lineage OS]]
- Support for a lot of devices
- Long-term support for old devices possible

** [[https://e.foundation/e-os/][/e/OS]]
- deGoogled by default
- Support for a lot of devices
- microG supported

** [[https://calyxos.org/][Calyx OS]] ([[https://calyxos.org/news/2025/08/01/a-letter-to-our-community/][Release paused]])
- Focus on user freedom
- deGoogled by default
- microG supported

** [[https://grapheneos.org/][Graphene OS]]
- Strong security
- Strong privacy
- deGoogled by default
- Profiles available
- microG supported
- Google dependency due to Pixel hardware (switch to Motorola)
